01 — About

I work on the hard part of agentic AI: knowing whether it actually worked.

At Microsoft, on GitHub Copilot, I build the evaluation machinery behind coding agents — a multi-axis intent taxonomy, LLM-as-judge classifiers holding 90%+ consistency per axis, and an outcome grader that ties offline benchmarks to what users actually experience.

That work leans on a habit of making expensive things cheap. I distilled a multi-turn intent classifier into a GNN that held its accuracy at roughly 2% of the original cost and latency, and analyzed ~100k Copilot sessions with the Office of the CTO to find where offline evaluation drifts from real usage.

Before that I built multimodal systems at Lily AI, and completed a MASc at the University of British Columbia on video diffusion models for echocardiogram generation — co-supervised by Prof. Renjie Liao and Prof. Purang Abolmaesumi, with work published at MICCAI and at workshops associated with CVPR and ICCV.
